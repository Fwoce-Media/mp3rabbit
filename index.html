An offline player, and nothing else

MP3 Rabbit plays the audio files already on your phone. It does not stream, it does not need an account, and nothing you listen to leaves your device.

Your library, organised

Songs, albums, artists, genres and playlists, read from the tags in your own files.

Your artwork

Covers come from the files themselves, so your library looks the way you made it.

Built for listening

A waveform seek bar, shuffle and repeat that stay put, and proper lock-screen controls.

Sound you can shape

Simple three-band tone, or the full graphic equaliser when you want it.

Light or dark

Follows your phone's theme, or pick the one you prefer and stay there.

No account, ever

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to, nothing uploaded.

Privacy

MP3 Rabbit does not collect your music, your listening history, or any personal information. Everything it knows about your library stays on your device.

The app is free and supported by advertising, and the advertising provider collects some information. What that is, and how to change your choices, is set out in full in the privacy policy.
